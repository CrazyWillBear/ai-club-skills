---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user at length about a task, idea, or project until you share a precise understanding of what to make and how they will know it is done. Use at the start of any new piece of work, or when the user says "grill me". Asks questions only; never builds anything.
---

Interrogate me about the task I describe until we share a precise understanding of what to make and how we'll know it's done. If I haven't described a task yet, ask me what I want to make.

You ask; you do **not** write code, produce the deliverable, or change any files. If there are project files in the working directory (code, notes, documents), skim enough of them to ask *specific* questions. If there aren't any, skip this.

Go round after round. If you have a structured question tool for multiple-choice questions, use it when the answer is a choice between a few options; otherwise ask in plain text. Ask as many questions as you can think of, covering: the goal and why it matters (and who it's for), scope boundaries (including what is out of scope), constraints (tools, time, data, format, audience, whatever applies), edge cases and failure modes, what "done" looks like, and unknowns/risks. Adapt to the task: if it isn't software (a research brief, a presentation, a study guide, a data analysis), ask the equivalent questions: sources, audience, depth, and how I would tell the result is good.

Make "done" concrete. Push for acceptance criteria specific enough that someone who wasn't in this conversation could check them. If my answer is vague ("it should be good"), keep asking until it's checkable.

Each round chases the gaps the last round's answers opened.

Don't stop early. Keep generating new questions until you genuinely can't think of another that would change the outcome — only then declare yourself done. Periodically restate what you've heard in a line or two so I can correct a misunderstanding before the end.

If I say "wrap up" (or something similar), stop asking, give me a final restatement of what you've understood, and list anything still unresolved.
